How this audit was built

The raw HTML could not be pulled. The audit environment's network policy blocks crownprincehall.com, Yelp, Eventective and the Wayback Machine. Findings come from what Google and Bing have indexed from the site and from third-party listings. Confirm three things before rollout:

  • View source on the homepage: the live title, meta description, and whether any application/ld+json block exists.
  • Run the Google Rich Results Test on the homepage.
  • Run PageSpeed Insights on mobile; a 360° tour embed usually drags the Largest Contentful Paint score and should be lazy-loaded.

Findings

HighThe site is a single page

Only the homepage is indexed. No wedding, quinceañera, corporate, party or pricing URL surfaces for any query, so one page is competing for five intents.

HighMeta description runs about 255 characters

Google cuts it mid-sentence at roughly 160. The indexed snippet ends at “…360° virtual tour, and 100+ gated parking.”

HighTitle leads with brand, not the query

Current: “Crown Prince Event Hall — San José Event & Banquet Venue”. “Banquet hall” and unaccented “San Jose” are not front-loaded and “wedding” is absent.

HighNo pricing page

A direct competitor, The Prestige Venue, ranks an indexed /pricing URL. Cost queries are the highest-intent searches in this category.

HighBusiness name is inconsistent

“Crown Prince Hall” in body copy versus “Crown Prince Event Hall” in the title and on Yelp. Name, address and phone must match everywhere.

MediumYelp category and hours

Yelp lists “Country Dance Halls” and “Open 24 hours”. Wrong categories dilute relevance and 24-hour hours look low quality to Google.

MediumNo FAQ block or FAQ schema

Policies exist as prose only. Question-and-answer format is what AI Overviews lift.

MediumThree competing calls to action

Book a tour, 360° tour and same-day quote share equal weight, with nothing for the researching majority.

LowAccented “San José” in the title

Google matches the forms, but the exact typed form wins SERP bolding. Keep the accent in brand copy; use “San Jose” in titles, metas, H1s and schema.

Snippet F · Connect, convert, close

Critique of the current calls to action

The indexed copy pushes three actions with equal weight: book a private tour, take the 360° tour, and get a same-day quote. A tour is a high-commitment ask that suits the small share of visitors comparing finalists. The 360° tour is engagement, not conversion, and leaves no contact record. The quote requires the visitor to compose a message. Nothing serves the majority who are still researching and want a price range and floor plan to share with a spouse, a parent or a committee. They leave and come back through a directory that charges for the lead.

Recommended hierarchy

  1. Primary on every page, hero and sticky mobile bar: “Get 2026 Pricing & Floor Plan”, opening the brochure form.
  2. Secondary: “Book a Private Tour”, a form that collects date, guest count and event type so the sales team qualifies before the visit.
  3. Tertiary, text link only: “Take the 360° Tour”.
  4. Header on mobile: click-to-call <a href="tel:+14084081654">(408) 408-1654</a>.
