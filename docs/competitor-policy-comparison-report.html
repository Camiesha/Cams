Evidence limit: venue websites could not be loaded in this environment, so competitor data is from third-party listings (The Knot, Zola, Wedding Spot, Tagvenue, Giggster) accessed 22 Oct 2026 — no competitor term is confirmed against an official venue document, and most venues do not publish contract terms at all.

Five priority improvements

  1. Separate reservation payment from refundable security deposit — the market norm; fixes ACD's four-in-one $1,000.
  2. Publish concrete overtime rates (competitors show $300–$500/hr) — both venues leave the field blank today.
  3. Adopt a graduated, rebooking-aware cancellation schedule designed to California law, since no competitor publishes one to copy.
  4. State booking hours and setup time unambiguously — resolve CPH's 6-hour-vs-setup contradiction.
  5. Make fee and price disclosure transparent — reconcile ACD's advertised price with its rate sheet; publish the CPH guest-count formula.

04

Best practices & policy gaps

Grouped for decision-making. Each adoption is supported by the evidence above; none is called an "industry standard" beyond what the data shows.

Worth adopting

  • Clear booking-confirmation requirement (signed + reservation payment)
  • Separate reservation payment and refundable security deposit
  • Transparent payment schedule and cancellation calculation
  • Documented damage deductions and stated refund timelines
  • Published overtime rate and clearly defined booking hours
  • Consistent insurance and vendor-document requirements

Clarify or correct

  • CPH setup-time contradiction (included vs added)
  • CPH guest-count pricing / fee formula undefined above 100
  • ACD $1,000 serving multiple purposes
  • ACD advertised price vs supplied rate sheet
  • Missing overtime rates (both)
  • ACD contracting entity & payment recipient
  • Unverified courtyard capacity; BYO-bar rules under-defined

Retain (already good)

  • CPH 14-day itemized security-deposit return
  • CPH insurance stack ($1M/$2M + host liquor, COI 30d)
  • CPH venue-bar model (keeps events licensed)
  • ACD graduated 3-step cancellation (closer to best practice)
  • ACD 1-year rescheduling credit
  • ACD prohibited-conduct & termination provisions
Why these, briefly. The adoptions close the gaps competitors reveal (refundable deposits, concrete overtime, transparent pricing) and the corrections remove the internal contradictions that cause wrong quotes and disputes. Items touching enforceability — cancellation percentages, waivers, surcharge wording, BYO-alcohol, accessibility — require California counsel; pricing and entity items require management.
