01 — Overview
Improvement proposal

CRM Follow-Up Enhancement

Every lead has a next step and a date. Automatic by default, editable when needed.

Today the Follow-Up column shows an automated status label. Labels are a judgment call, and the system's judgment does not always match what happened with the client. This proposal turns the column into a scheduled next step: what to do, when, and who owns it. The CRM still sets it automatically after every recorded interaction. When the system gets it wrong, an authorized user changes the date or marks the step done directly from the Leads list, and that edit is protected from being silently overwritten.

03 — Proposal

A next step and a date, not a label.

Replace the status label with a scheduled follow-up: an action, a due date and an owner. The CRM sets it automatically. Authorized users adjust it when needed.

The key rule

A date or step set by a person is not overwritten by the next automated update. It remains protected until one of two things happens:

  1. The authorized user changes it again or marks it done, or
  2. A clearly new client interaction is recorded, according to the agreed CRM logic.

06 — Editing

Editing a follow-up

Four actions, all inside the Leads list. No separate screen, no form to fill.

Step 1Review the list
Step 2Spot a wrong date or step
Step 3Adjust, or mark done
Step 4Save

What the user sees

The advisor opens the Follow-Up value on the row. They can move the date, change the action, add a short note, or mark the step done and record what happened. The column immediately shows the new step with a small Set by line: who changed it and when.

  • Marking a step done asks for one outcome: Reached, No response, Waiting for client, or Booked / closed.
  • The CRM then schedules the next step automatically by the agreed rule, unless the outcome is Booked / closed.
  • Only authorized users see the edit control. Everyone else sees the same read-only value.
  • Every manual change is visible on the row, so managers can tell at a glance which schedules a person adjusted.

07 — Impact

Why this matters

The Follow-Up column is where the team decides who to contact next. A date makes that decision for them.

01

Fewer missed follow-ups

Every open lead has a due date. Anything past it turns Overdue on its own and rises to the top of the list.

02

Less duplicate outreach

Clients already reached by phone or in person are marked done with an outcome, so nobody contacts them again by mistake.

03

Faster lead review

Sort by due date and work top down. Managers see overdue and due-today counts without opening a single record.

04

More reliable CRM data

Outcomes, dates and who set them are recorded, so reports on follow-up activity reflect what happened.

08 — Scope

What stays the same

This proposal is deliberately narrow. It changes how one column behaves and nothing else.

Only this changes Follow-Up column behavior

The column shows a scheduled next step instead of a status label. Automation still sets it. Authorized users can adjust it, with a visible source indicator and protection from automatic overwrite.
