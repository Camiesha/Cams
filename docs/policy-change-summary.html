Policy Change Summary — CPH & ACD

The proposed revised deposit and cancellation policy for Crown Prince Event Hall and Alvin Studio & Courtyard, a side-by-side comparison of the current versus proposed terms, the competitor practices and sources that informed it, and the decisions still needing sign-off.

PROPOSED DRAFT — FOR MANAGEMENT REVIEW AND CALIFORNIA LEGAL COUNSEL REVIEW — NOT APPROVED FOR CLIENT USE
1

Proposed revised policy

The complete revised wording is built into the two rental agreements (companion crown-prince-rental-agreement and alvin-studio-rental-agreement). The core terms:

Initial reservation deposit

  • CPH $1,000 · ACD $500, required to secure the date.
  • Credited toward the rental total in the approved quotation — not an additional charge; deducted in the payment summary.
  • It is not a security deposit and does not convert into one.

Cancellation of the deposit

  • Within 14 calendar days of paying (in writing) → full refund (CPH $1,000; ACD $500).
  • After 14 days → 20% refunded, 80% retained (CPH $200/$800; ACD $100/$400), subject to the final agreement and law.
  • Each cancellation reviewed individually; retained amounts are not automatically classified as profit.
Deliberately not decided here: the refund treatment of payments beyond the deposit, the refund processing deadline, and rescheduling terms are left for management and California counsel — no schedule is invented.

4

Items needing management decision or legal review

Management confirmation

  • Refund treatment of payments beyond the deposit
  • Refund processing deadline; reminder schedule
  • Rescheduling / date-change terms
  • Whether a separate security/damage deposit is collected (amount, return)
  • ACD legal entity & payment recipient; ACD pricing model & amounts
  • Overtime rate & grace (each venue); balance deadline (14 vs 30)
  • CASp status for each premises; applicable sales tax per charge
  • ACD insurance limits, guard ratio, courtyard capacity / weather / outdoor sound

California legal counsel review

  • 20% / 80% retention as liquidated damages (Civ. Code §1671)
  • Release / §1542 waiver / indemnification scope; liability limitation
  • Replace the 1%/month interest; remove the no-dispute clause
  • Credit-card surcharge wording; debit exclusion
  • Alcohol / ABC (ACD BYO); "no refund if ended for breach" proportionality
  • CASp §1938 disclosure wording; force-majeure remedy
Do not treat any of this as approved or enforceable. Existing bookings keep their current terms; the new policy applies only to bookings made after an approved effective date. Full registers: companion venue-policy-redesign-package (D8–D9) and the agreements' appendix.
