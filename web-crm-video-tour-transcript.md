### 0:00 — Title
Here's a quick tour of the Web CRM — how one real lead becomes a booking. Let's follow Marisela.

### 0:07 — The big picture
First, the big picture. We run two venues — Crown Prince Event Hall, up to three hundred guests, and Alvin Studio and Courtyard. Every day, people enquire about weddings, quinceañeras, debuts, birthdays and more — and the CRM turns each enquiry into a lead, and follows it all the way to a booked event.

### 0:19 — Step 1 / 10 · Leads list
It starts in the Leads list — the whole team's shared view. Every enquiry shows its source, who owns it, its status, and the next step. We open Marisela's lead.

### 0:32 — Step 2 / 10 · Lead profile & Journey
Her Journey is right at the top. A Lead on October second, Contacted the same day, a tour on the fourth, a proposal, and her deposit on the fourteenth — Won. Click a stage and the lead moves there.

### 0:47 — Step 3 / 10 · Communications
We reach out without leaving the lead — Text or Email — and the whole conversation stays attached, so anyone can pick it up.

### 0:59 — Step 4 / 10 · Interaction Recap
After a follow-up call or a text, we write up an interaction recap. Type what the customer liked, what worried them, and what we agreed to do next — or just dictate it, and the CRM writes it up. Press Save, and it's logged on the lead, so the next person knows exactly what happened.

### 1:14 — Step 5 / 10 · Quotation
Next, the quotation. The CRM builds it on the venue's template — pricing, event date and guest count — and we send it to the customer with a single click. A seventy-two-hour deposit link rides along with it.

### 1:28 — Step 6 / 10 · Deposit / Payments
When the deposit comes in, it shows up here in Payments. Marisela paid a thousand dollars — two five-hundred-dollar Zelle transfers — each checked against the bank, and a receipt goes back to her automatically.

### 1:42 — Step 7 / 10 · Rental agreement
With the deposit in, the last step is the rental agreement. We send it for e-signature right from the booking — and the date confirms the moment the signed agreement is on file.

### 1:56 — Step 8 / 10 · Bookings
Zoom out, and the Bookings page shows the whole portfolio at once — booked value, collected, outstanding, and holds — with the next action on every one, so nothing stalls quietly.

### 2:09 — Step 9 / 10 · Accounting
And the Accounting page shows the whole money picture — billed, collected, outstanding, and what's overdue — with the things to fix today.

### 2:22 — Closing
That's the Web CRM — one lead, one shared record, from first enquiry, to booked and paid.
