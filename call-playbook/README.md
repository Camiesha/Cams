# Crown Prince Call Script & Scenario Playbook

A polished, internal training document for **Crown Prince Event Hall** Client
Coordinators — covering incoming leads, outbound calls, follow-ups, tour
scheduling, pricing inquiries, objections, and booking conversion.

## File

- **`playbook.html`** — the complete playbook. Open in any browser. Single,
  self-contained HTML file (no build step, no external assets beyond Google
  Fonts). Light/dark theme aware.

## What's inside

1. Cover + sticky table of contents
2. Venue overview & verified pricing reference (source of truth)
3. Standard discovery flow + sample discovery conversation
4. **84 word-for-word scenarios** across 8 sets:
   - A · New lead & initial contact (1–10)
   - B · Lead qualification (11–20)
   - C · Pricing & quote inquiries (21–32)
   - D · Event-specific conversations (33–40)
   - E · Venue policies & facilities (41–52)
   - F · Tour scheduling & follow-up (53–62)
   - G · Objections & difficult conversations (63–74)
   - H · Closing & reservation conversion (75–84)
5. Call flow & decision tree (Mermaid)
6. Proposed follow-up sequence + ready-to-use messages
7. Training cheat sheets + 5 role-play exercises
8. Supervisor quality-control checklist + proposed scoring
9. Policy verification & escalation matrix

## Design & conventions

- Brand palette: forest green `#2F5D4A` on cream `#F6F3EC`.
- Type system matches the rest of this project (Bodoni Moda / Source Sans 3 /
  IBM Plex Mono).
- Each scenario cleanly separates **what you say** (green *Say* blocks) from
  **internal guidance**, **CRM actions**, and **Verify-first** reminders.
- CRM pipeline stages mirror the live Web CRM: Lead → Contacted → Tour →
  Proposal → Won. Where a sub-status isn't standardized, the document says
  "use the corresponding approved CRM status."

## Accuracy rules baked into the content

- Published **starting** prices ($3,845 Mon–Thu · $4,845 Fri–Sun & holidays)
  are never presented as final totals.
- No invented discounts, taxes, extra-hour prices, deposit amounts, refund or
  cancellation terms, or payment methods.
- No availability, tour time, quote, or booking is ever stated as confirmed
  before it is actually checked/verified via the approved workflow.
- References only real Crown Prince features (no courtyard/studio).

> Starting prices and published policies are current as supplied. Anything not
> published must be verified with management before being quoted to a client.
